# Overview

Snap Pose follows a Freemium business model.

Users can enjoy the core experience for free while Premium unlocks advanced features, exclusive content, and an ad-free experience.

The monetization strategy is designed to maximize long-term revenue while complying with Google Play and AdMob policies.
