# Brand Overview

**POSEHANUM** is an AI-powered photography & pose assistant that helps users capture stunning, natural photos using real-time pose matching, voice coaching, and smart camera technology.

### Brand Meaning:
"Hanum" is inspired by the Nepali expression for *"let's do/take it."*  
**POSEHANUM** = *"Let's take the pose / Let's capture the shot."*

### Core Brand Promise:
*"POSEHANUM guides you from 'How should I pose?' to 'That's the shot.'"*

### Primary Tagline:
**"Pose Garौँ. Perfect Shot Lिऔँ."**

### English Tagline:
**"Let's Pose. Let's Capture."**

### Secondary Tagline:
**"Pose Smarter. Capture Better."**

Brand Personality:
- Playful & Clever
- Confident & Photography-Aware
- Minimal & Premium
- Modern & Gen-Z Friendly
- Intelligent & Trustworthy
- Fast & Responsive
