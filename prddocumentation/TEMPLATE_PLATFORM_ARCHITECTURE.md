## 1. Product Loop

```
DISCOVER TEMPLATE ➔ VIEW SHOT RECIPE ➔ REMIX POSE ➔ CAMERA ALIGNMENT ➔ AI DIRECTOR COACHING ➔ AUTO CAPTURE ➔ STUDIO CANVAS EDIT ➔ EXPORT & COMMUNITY SHARE
```

---

## 2. Multi-Layer Canvas Model

- **Layer 1: Background**: Color, gradient, or scene backdrop.
- **Layer 2: Pose Silhouette Reference**: Translucent target reference overlay.
- **Layer 3: User Photo Capture**: Captured image.
- **Layer 4: Stickers & Emojis**: Draggable, scalable, rotatable overlay stickers.
- **Layer 5: Typography & Captions**: Custom font styling, color, opacity, alignment.

---

## 3. Shot Recipe Specification

Every template includes:
- **Pose**: Reference title & landmarks
- **Camera Angle**: Eye level / low angle / high angle
- **Distance**: 1m, 2m, 3m
- **Lighting**: Natural, side light, golden hour, backlight
- **Expression**: Confident, relaxed, cinematic
- **Framing**: Full body, medium shot, close up
- **Director Tip**: Spoken coaching instruction
