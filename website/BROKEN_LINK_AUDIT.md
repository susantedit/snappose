## 3. Findings & Resolution Summary
- **Before**: Subpages linked directly to relative hash links (e.g. `href="#how-it-works"`), causing broken navigation when clicked from `/privacy` or `/blog`.
- **After**: All header and footer links updated to absolute domain paths (`href="/#how-it-works"`, `href="/#faq"`), ensuring smooth multi-page navigation across all subpages.
